## 📌 About the Project

**KK Automotive Private Limited** is an automotive service and mechanical solutions platform dedicated to providing reliable vehicle maintenance, professional repair services, and quality-tested automotive components.

The platform focuses on delivering comprehensive automotive care, from engine diagnostics and transmission repairs to braking systems, electrical services, suspension maintenance, and air-conditioning solutions.

Our mission is to keep every vehicle safe, efficient, and road-ready through expert workmanship, transparent pricing, and dependable automotive solutions.

## 🎯 Key Objectives

- Provide comprehensive automobile repair and maintenance services.
- Offer genuine and quality-tested automotive spare parts.
- Deliver professional vehicle diagnostics and mechanical solutions.
- Maintain transparency in service estimates and pricing.
- Improve customer convenience through online service booking.
- Enable customers to manage their vehicle service history.
